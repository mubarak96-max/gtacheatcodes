## What is the GTA San Andreas money cheat?

The GTA San Andreas money cheat is **HESOYAM**. Enter it during gameplay and you instantly get **$250,000**, plus full health, full armor and a repaired vehicle. It's the only cheat in San Andreas that gives you money, and it's probably the single most-used code in the whole game.

That combo is why HESOYAM is so popular. It's not just a cash code, it's an emergency button. Car on fire? Health nearly gone? Can't afford the next property? One code fixes all three.

## How to use the money cheat, step by step

1. Load your save and get into normal gameplay (not paused, not in a menu).
2. **PC:** type `HESOYAM`. No Enter key.
3. **Console:** press the sequence above quickly, using the d-pad for directions.
4. Wait for **"Cheat activated"**. Your cash goes up by $250,000.
5. Want more? Enter it again.

## Can you use HESOYAM repeatedly?

Yes. There's no cooldown and no limit on how many times you enter it. Each entry adds another $250,000. Four entries gets you a million dollars; enter it a few dozen times and money stops being a concern for the rest of the game.

A few practical points:

- **Wait for the confirmation each time.** Starting the next entry before the message appears can mean one doesn't register.
- **It can't save you after the fact.** HESOYAM heals you but can't undo a death that's already happened. Use it before things go wrong.
- **It doesn't fix everything.** It repairs the vehicle you're in, not every car around you, and it won't clear your wanted level. For that, use `TURNDOWNTHEHEAT` (or `ASNAEB`) on PC.

## How to make money in San Andreas without cheats

Prefer to earn it? San Andreas has plenty of legitimate ways to make cash. Exact payouts vary, so we've stuck to the reliable methods rather than quoting precise figures.

### Story missions

The main story pays out steadily, and the later chapters pay considerably more than the Los Santos missions. Simply progressing the story will cover most of what you need, including the required property purchases.

### Assets and businesses

Several businesses in San Andreas become "assets" once you complete their related missions. Assets generate income over time that you collect from a pickup at the property. Examples include the RS Haul trucking company, Hunter Quarry, the Verdant Meadows airstrip and the Wang Cars showroom in San Fierro. Finishing their mission strings is one of the best long-term money moves in the game.

### Gang territory in Los Santos

Taking and holding gang territory for the Grove Street Families generates money over time. It's not a fortune on its own, but it adds up, and the gang wars are fun anyway.

### Side jobs

Vehicle side missions pay as you go and often unlock perks:

- **Taxi, Paramedic, Firefighter and Vigilante** missions pay per stage.
- **Burglary** (night time only, in a burglary van) pays for stolen goods you drop off.
- **Pimping**, **Courier** and **Trucking** jobs all add to your income.

### Betting on Inside Track

Off-track betting shops in San Andreas let you bet on horse races through **Inside Track**. Odds and outcomes are random, so it's a gamble. Save before you try it.

### Casinos in Las Venturas

Las Venturas has casinos with blackjack, roulette, video poker, slots and the Wheel of Fortune. You can win a lot quickly and lose it faster. Two tips:

- **Save before you sit down**, then reload if it goes badly. That's the closest thing to a guaranteed win.
- **Collecting the horseshoes** hidden around Las Venturas improves CJ's luck, which helps at the tables, though gambling stays a gamble.

### Collectibles and challenges

Finishing whole sets of collectibles, such as the spray tags in Los Santos, unlocks weapon pickups at your safehouses. Free guns mean fewer paid trips to Ammu-Nation, which is money saved.

## What should you spend HESOYAM money on?

Once cash stops being a problem, the fun part is spending it. A few ideas that make a real difference to your playthrough:

- **Safehouses across the map.** Buying properties in every city gives you more save points, which means less driving back to save after a mission.
- **Body armor and weapons from Ammu-Nation.** Even with cheat weapon sets, buying the exact gun you want keeps your loadout tidy.
- **Clothes, haircuts and tattoos.** These affect CJ's look and his sex appeal stat, which matters for girlfriends.
- **Car modifications.** TransFender, Loco Low Co. and Wheel Arch Angels let you tune and customise cars. Nitro, hydraulics and paint jobs all cost money.
- **Gym memberships and food.** Shaping CJ's body the legitimate way still costs a little, but nothing compared with a quarter of a million dollars.
