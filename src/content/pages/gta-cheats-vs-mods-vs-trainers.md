## What's the difference between GTA cheats, mods and trainers?

**Cheats are official. Mods and trainers are not.** That is the core difference. A GTA cheat is something Rockstar deliberately put in the game: you enter a code and the game does something it was programmed to do, like spawn a tank or drop your wanted level. A mod is something made by fans that changes the game itself. A trainer is a specific kind of mod that gives you a menu of powers, a bit like having every cheat imaginable on one screen.

The three overlap in what they can achieve (god mode, free cars, crazy physics) but differ hugely in how they work, which platforms support them, and how safe they are. Here is the plain-English breakdown.

## Definitions at a glance

| | Cheats | Mods | Trainers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Made by | Rockstar | Fans and modders | Fans and modders |
| What it is | Built-in codes | Changes to game files or code | A mod that adds a powers menu |
| How you use it | Buttons, phone numbers or PC console | Install files on PC | Install, then open with a hotkey |
| Platforms | All platforms the game runs on | Mostly PC | Mostly PC |
| Single player | Fully supported | Generally tolerated | Generally tolerated |
| GTA Online | Do not work | Ban risk | Ban risk |
| Achievements | Disabled for the session (in games that have them) | Varies | Varies |

## What are GTA cheats?

**A GTA cheat is a built-in code that triggers an effect the developers programmed into the game.** You enter them in one of three ways, depending on the game and platform:

- **Button combos** on console, like `R1 R2 L1 X L D R U L D R U` for San Andreas's HESOYAM on PlayStation.
- **Phone numbers** in GTA IV and GTA 5, like `1-999-887-853` for GTA 5's max health and armor.
- **Typed codes** on PC, like `PAINKILLER` in GTA 5's console or `PANZER` typed during gameplay in Vice City.

Cheats have been part of the series since the very first games, and the [history of GTA cheat codes](/guides/history-of-gta-cheat-codes/) is a fun rabbit hole if you are curious how we got from top-down codes to 1-999 numbers.

### Pros of cheats

- **Official and safe.** Nothing to download, no risk of malware.
- **Work on consoles.** PlayStation, Xbox, PC, and in some games Switch and mobile.
- **Easy to undo.** Most effects are temporary or toggle off.
- **No install, no updates breaking anything.** They survive patches because they are part of the game.

### Cons of cheats

- **Limited menu.** You get what Rockstar gave you. GTA 5 cannot spawn every car, and there is no money cheat (see the [GTA 5 money cheat guide](/guides/gta-5-money-cheat/)).
- **Achievements are disabled** for the session in games that have them.
- **A few cheats can damage old saves**, especially in San Andreas. Read [do cheats disable saves?](/guides/do-cheats-disable-saves-gta/) before going wild.

## What are GTA mods?

**A mod (modification) is a fan-made change to the game's files or code.** Mods range from tiny tweaks to total overhauls:

- **Visual mods** such as texture packs, lighting changes and graphics enhancements.
- **Vehicle and character mods** that add new cars, outfits or player models.
- **Gameplay mods** that change police behavior, damage, handling or AI.
- **Script mods** that add new features, missions or systems.
- **Total conversions** that rebuild large parts of the game.

Mods mostly live on PC, because PC lets you change game files. Many GTA 5 mods rely on community tools such as script hooks and asset managers, which are themselves fan-made.

### Pros of mods

- **Nearly unlimited possibilities.** If someone has imagined it, there is probably a mod for it.
- **Can improve the game** with better visuals, quality-of-life fixes or new content.
- **Huge, creative community** keeps older GTAs alive years after release.

### Cons of mods

- **PC only, for practical purposes.**
- **Game updates often break mods** until they are updated, and some never are.
- **Compatibility headaches.** Mods can conflict with each other.
- **Security risk** if you download from shady sources.
- **Not allowed in GTA Online.**

## What are GTA trainers?

**A trainer is a mod that gives you an in-game menu packed with cheat-like options.** Open it with a hotkey and you can usually:

- Spawn almost any vehicle, weapon or character
- Teleport anywhere on the map
- Give yourself god mode, infinite ammo or no wanted level
- Change time, weather and gravity
- Edit money and stats in single player

Think of a trainer as the official cheat list with the limits removed. Instead of the ten or so vehicles GTA 5's cheats can spawn, a trainer lets you pick from the entire vehicle list.

### Pros of trainers

- **Far more powerful than built-in cheats.**
- **Great for creators,** for example people who make machinima, screenshots or stunt videos.
- **Convenient.** No memorizing codes.

### Cons of trainers

- **Installation required,** usually alongside other modding tools.
- **Can corrupt saves** if you edit stats or story flags carelessly.
- **Ban risk if they are active in GTA Online.** A trainer that works in story mode can get you flagged the moment you load Online.
- **Updates break them,** and the newer GTA V Enhanced PC edition may need different versions of tools than Legacy.

## Are trainers the same as mod menus?

They are closely related but used differently. "Trainer" usually means a **single-player** tool. "Mod menu" is the term most often used for tools designed to cheat **in GTA Online**, which is against Rockstar's rules and can get accounts suspended, reset or banned. Our [GTA Online cheats guide](/guides/gta-online-cheats/) covers why mod menus are a bad idea, including the risk of malware and scams.

## Is modding GTA legal?

Short answer: for single-player use, modding is generally treated as a tolerated grey area rather than a crime, but it is governed by Rockstar's and Take-Two's terms, not by you. Over the years, Rockstar and Take-Two have generally indicated that they do not target single-player PC mods, while firmly opposing anything that affects GTA Online. That stance can change and is not a legal guarantee.

What is clearly off-limits:

- **Using mods, trainers or menus in GTA Online.**
- **Selling or distributing Rockstar's copyrighted files.**
- **Cheating services** that sell money, accounts or unlocks.

Using official cheats, on the other hand, is completely fine. They are a feature of the game.

## Safety checklist before you mod

1. **Back up your saves** and, ideally, your game folder.
2. **Download only from well-known community sites** with moderation, comments and download counts.
3. **Avoid executables from random links,** especially anything advertised as "free money" or "undetected".
4. **Never enter your Rockstar, Steam, Epic, PSN or Xbox login** into a mod or trainer.
5. **Disable or remove mods before playing GTA Online,** or keep a separate clean install.
6. **Read the mod's instructions** and check it supports your game version (Legacy or Enhanced for GTA 5 on PC).

## When should you use cheats, mods or trainers?

| You want to... | Best choice |
|---|---|
| Mess around for ten minutes on console | Cheats |
| Escape the cops during a story mission | Cheats |
| Make GTA look better on PC | Mods |
| Add new cars or new missions | Mods |
| Spawn any vehicle or teleport freely in story mode | Trainer |
| Shoot cinematic videos or screenshots | Trainer |
| Get ahead in GTA Online | None. Play legitimately |

## The bottom line

Cheats are the safe, official option and work on every platform. Mods change the game itself and are the playground of PC players. Trainers are a supercharged, unofficial cheat menu for single player. All three are fun in story mode. None of them belong in GTA Online.
