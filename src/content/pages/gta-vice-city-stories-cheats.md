## What are GTA Vice City Stories cheats?

GTA Vice City Stories cheats are button combinations you enter mid-game to get money, weapons, vehicles, weather changes and chaos in Rockstar's 1984-set prequel to Vice City. You play as Victor "Vic" Vance, and every cheat is an eight-button sequence pressed while you play. The game flashes a confirmation when a code lands.

VCS released on the PSP in 2006 and came to the PS2 in early 2007. Unlike Liberty City Stories, it never got an iOS, Android or PC version, so these PlayStation button codes are the whole story. There are no typed words and no phone numbers.

This is an unofficial fan page with no connection to Rockstar Games or Take-Two. We only list codes that at least two established GTA references agree on. Where sources disagree about which version a code works on, we say so instead of guessing.

## How to enter GTA VCS cheats

1. Load a save and get into normal gameplay, on foot or in a vehicle.
2. Stay out of the pause menu, map and phone screens.
3. Press the eight inputs in order using the d-pad, not the analog stick.
4. Look for the on-screen confirmation. If it does not appear, restart the sequence from the first button.
5. Re-enter stackable cheats, such as money or weapons, as many times as you need.

Tip: VCS cheats mix d-pad and face buttons constantly. Saying the sequence out loud ("up, down, left, right, cross, cross, L, R") genuinely helps.

## PSP vs PS2: what changes?

For the shared codes, the only difference is the shoulder buttons. The PSP has a single L and a single R, so every L1 in a PS2 code becomes L on PSP and every R1 becomes R. VCS cheats never use L2 or R2.

Beyond that, a few codes are documented for one version only:

- **PSP:** Black Traffic, plus four multiplayer unlock codes for the handheld's wireless modes.
- **PS2:** Guy Magnet, Passenger Pedestrian and Aggressive Traffic. One major database lists these for PSP too, but the two big GTA wikis only document them on PS2, so treat them as PS2 codes unless you confirm otherwise in your copy.

One small control difference matters with the Perfect Handling cheat, which lets cars hop: press L3 to jump on PS2 and Down on the d-pad on PSP.

## Will cheats break my VCS save?

Cheats are not blocked from saving in VCS, which is exactly why you need to be careful. Saving with a permanent cheat such as Pedestrians Riot locks that chaos into the file. Keep one clean save slot that never sees a cheat, and experiment in another. Our [guide to cheats and saves](/guides/do-cheats-disable-saves-gta/) covers how this works across the series.
