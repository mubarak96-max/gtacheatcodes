## How do GTA San Andreas PC cheats work?

GTA San Andreas PC cheats work by typing: while you're playing, you type a string of letters on your keyboard and the game listens for it. There's no console to open, no command box, no menu and no Enter key. Type `HESOYAM` while CJ is walking down Grove Street and you'll get full health, full armor, a repaired vehicle and $250,000 the instant you hit the final M.

That's very different from GTA V on PC, where you open a console with the tilde key and press Enter (see our [GTA 5 PC cheats guide](/guides/gta-5-pc-cheats/) if that's what you were expecting). San Andreas is old school. The game quietly keeps track of the last keys you pressed and, when they spell a known code, it fires.

## How to enter a cheat in GTA San Andreas on PC, step by step

1. **Load a save or start a new game.** Cheats can't be entered from the main menu.
2. **Get into normal gameplay.** On foot or in a vehicle is fine. Close the pause menu, the map and any shop or garage screens.
3. **Type the code** using the letter keys on your keyboard. For example, `AEZAKMI` for never wanted.
4. **Look for "Cheat activated"** in the top corner of the screen. That message is your only confirmation.
5. **If nothing happens, start again from the first letter.** There's no backspace and no partial credit.

You don't have to type at a frantic pace, but don't pause halfway through either. Moving CJ while typing is fine as long as the movement keys don't land inside the code. If your movement keys are WASD, letters like A, S and D in a code will also move CJ. That's normal and harmless.

## What does "Cheat activated" mean?

"Cheat activated" is the on-screen text San Andreas shows when it recognises a code. If you see it, the effect has been applied, even if it's not obvious yet. Weather cheats can take a moment to fade in, and traffic cheats only affect cars that spawn after you entered them.

If you don't see it, the game didn't register the code. There's no error message for a wrong code; the game simply ignores keystrokes that don't match anything.

## Letter keys vs numpad: which keys do you use?

San Andreas PC codes are made of letters only, so you type them on the main letter keys. The numpad isn't used for any San Andreas cheat. Some numpad keys are bound to vehicle controls by default, which is another reason to stay on the letters.

Capitalisation doesn't matter. The game reads key presses, not characters, so Caps Lock on or off makes no difference. The codes on this page are written in capitals purely so they're easy to read.

One thing that can matter is your **keyboard layout**. The codes were designed around a standard QWERTY keyboard. Some players using AZERTY or QWERTZ layouts report that codes only work when they press the keys in the positions they'd occupy on a QWERTY keyboard. If you're on a non-QWERTY layout and nothing works, try switching your system layout to US English while you play.

## The original 2005 PC version vs the Definitive Edition on PC

San Andreas has been on PC more than once. Cheats behave similarly across them, with a few differences worth knowing.

### The original PC release

The original 2005 PC version is the classic typing experience described above. Later PC releases of the original game kept the same keyboard cheat system. Rockstar has re-released and patched the PC version over the years, so if a specific code misbehaves on your copy, check that you're entering it during normal gameplay before blaming the version.

### The Definitive Edition on PC

The Definitive Edition, released in 2021, rebuilt the game in a new engine and changed plenty of things, but the classic codes remain. On PC you type them during gameplay just like the original. If you play the Definitive Edition with a controller and typing doesn't seem to respond, entering the console-style button sequence on your gamepad is worth a try; the Xbox layout is shown in the table at the top of this page.

The bigger Definitive Edition difference is unlocks: using cheats can prevent achievements from unlocking. Keep a clean save if achievements matter to you. The [Definitive Edition cheats hub](/gta-trilogy-definitive-edition-cheats/) covers the remaster in more depth.

## Common San Andreas PC cheat typos

Most "broken" cheats are actually typos. These are the ones we see most often:

| You typed | You meant | What went wrong |
|---|---|---|
| HESOYAN | HESOYAM | Last letter is M, not N |
| BAGUVIK | BAGUVIX | Ends in X |
| AEZAKIM | AEZAKMI | The last three are K-M-I |
| PROFESSIONALKIT | PROFESSIONALSKIT | There's an S before KIT |
| ROCKETMEN | ROCKETMAN | Singular, one man |
| TURNDOWNHEAT | TURNDOWNTHEHEAT | Every word counts, including THE |
| NATURALTALANT | NATURALTALENT | Spelled like the English word |

Watch out for these near misses too:

- **PROFESSIONALSKIT vs PROFESSIONALKILLER.** The first is Weapon Set 2; the second maxes every weapon skill to Hitman level. Both are real, and they do different things.
- **Vice City codes.** Vice City codes like PANZER don't work in San Andreas. The tank here is `AIWPRTON`.
- **GTA V codes.** Words like PAINKILLER belong to GTA V's console, not San Andreas.

## Why else might a PC cheat fail?

If you're sure the spelling is right, run through this checklist:

1. **Is the game paused or a menu open?** Close it and try again.
2. **Did you start typing mid-word?** If you pressed a stray key first, the code may still work, but retyping cleanly costs nothing.
3. **Is another program grabbing your keys?** Overlays and macro tools can occasionally interfere. Close them and retry.
4. **Is the cheat situational?** Vehicle spawns need a bit of open space and may not work in some interiors or during certain missions.
5. **Is it a toggle?** Many cheats switch on and off. If you typed it twice, you may have turned it back off.
