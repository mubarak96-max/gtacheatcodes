## Which GTA San Andreas cheats ruin your save?

The GTA San Andreas cheats that can ruin your save are the ones that change the world rather than just helping CJ: the two clock-freezing cheats, the pedestrian chaos cheats and Blow Up All Cars. None of them will typically corrupt your save file. The problem is what happens if you **save while they're active** and then try to carry on with the story.

## Why do the time-freezing cheats cause problems?

**Always Midnight** and **Orange Sky** stop the in-game clock. That sounds cosmetic, and for a screenshot session it is. The catch is that San Andreas uses time for a lot of things:

- **Some activities only run at certain hours.** Burglary, for example, only works at night. A clock stuck at one hour means anything restricted to other hours may not be available.
- **Some things need days to pass.** Events that wait for time to move on, and timed objectives within missions, can behave unpredictably or simply never progress when the clock isn't moving.
- **The lighting never changes.** Permanent midnight makes driving and spotting enemies harder for the whole session.

If you save with the clock frozen, you can load back in to the same frozen state. Entering the cheat again is often enough to undo it, but behaviour can differ between versions, so don't rely on it. The safest route is not to save with a time cheat active at all.

The related **Faster Clock** cheat (`YSOHNUL`) isn't flagged as risky, but it makes timed parts of the game feel very different. Treat it with the same caution if you're mid-story.

## Why are the riot and gang cheats risky?

The pedestrian cheats change how everyone on the street behaves:

- **Pedestrians Riot** (`STATEOFEMERGENCY`) makes pedestrians fight each other. Cars get wrecked, fires break out and the streets become a war zone.
- **Pedestrians Attack You** (`BAGOWPG`) points all of that aggression at CJ.
- **Gang Members Everywhere** (`MROEMZH`) fills the streets with gang members, many of whom will be hostile to CJ.

These are fantastic in free roam. In a story mission they can be a nightmare: escort targets get attacked, stealth sections become impossible to keep quiet and chases turn into demolition derbies. Players have long reported that these effects can be hard to shake once saved into a file, so treat them as save-and-reload cheats: use them on a throwaway save, then load your clean one.

**Pedestrians Have Weapons** (`FOOOXFT`) and **Aggressive Traffic** (`YLTEICZ`) aren't flagged as risky in our data, but they push in the same direction. If you're about to start a delicate mission, switch them off first.

## Why is Blow Up All Cars dangerous?

**Blow Up All Cars** (`CPKTNWT`) instantly destroys every vehicle near CJ. That includes:

- The car you're sitting in.
- Any vehicle a mission needs you to deliver, protect or follow.
- Cars with your gang members or mission characters inside.

Lose a mission-critical vehicle and the mission fails. Since the cheat hits everything nearby at once, it's very easy to blow up something important without noticing. Use it freely in free roam, never during a mission.

## Other cheats to think twice about

Not every risky cheat is flagged. A few more that can make life awkward:

- **Suicide** (`SZCMAWO`) kills CJ instantly, failing any active mission.
- **Six-Star Wanted Level** (`BRINGITON`) and **Raise Wanted Level** (`OSRBLHH`) bring massive police and military attention. Fun, but not mid-mission.
- **Max Fat** (`BTCDBCB`) and **Skinny CJ** (`KVGYZQK`) change CJ's body stats. That's not save-breaking, but you'll have to eat or exercise to change them back, so think about it before you save.
- **Weather cheats** like Sandstorm (`CWJXUOC`) or Foggy Weather (`CFVFGMJ`) reduce visibility. They're harmless long term but can make a mission harder in the moment.

## Is it true that cheats corrupt San Andreas saves?

Mostly, no. Rumours that a single code will destroy your save file have circulated for years, but the realistic problems are the ones on this page: you save while the world is in a strange state and then have to deal with that state later. In The Definitive Edition there's a separate consideration: using cheats can affect trophy or achievement unlocks. ## How to protect your San Andreas save: best practice

Follow these steps and cheats can't really hurt you:

1. **Make a clean save before cheating.** Save in a new slot you'll never overwrite while cheating.
2. **Use a separate cheat slot.** Do all your experimenting in a second slot. Save there as often as you like.
3. **Clear chaos cheats before saving.** If you must save, re-enter toggle cheats to switch them off first and confirm the clock is moving and the streets are normal.
4. **Never save mid-chaos.** If a riot is raging or the clock looks stuck, don't save, even in your cheat slot, unless you're happy to keep that state.
5. **Back up the files on PC.** PC players can copy their save files to another folder before a big session. Our [San Andreas PC cheats guide](/guides/gta-san-andreas-pc-cheats/) has more PC-specific tips.
6. **Avoid cheats during missions.** Use them between missions, in free roam, where failure costs nothing.

## What if you've already saved with a risky cheat?

Don't panic. Try these in order:

1. **Re-enter the cheat.** Many cheats are toggles, so entering the same code again may switch the effect off.
2. **Look for a counter-cheat.** A weather cheat like Sunny Weather (`AFZLLQLL`) can replace another weather effect.
3. **Load an older save.** If you kept a clean slot, this is where it pays off.
4. **Sleep or wait at a safehouse** to see whether time starts moving again. Results vary, so don't count on it with a frozen clock.
