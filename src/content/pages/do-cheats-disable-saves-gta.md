## Do cheats disable saving in GTA?

**No. No mainline GTA game stops you from saving because you used cheats.** You can enter PAINKILLER in GTA 5, HESOYAM in San Andreas or PANZER in Vice City and still walk into a safehouse and save. That is the simple answer most people are searching for.

The longer answer is that cheats can affect two other things: **your achievements and trophies**, and **the contents of your save file itself**. Newer games mostly worry about the first. Older games mostly risk the second. Which one you need to care about depends entirely on which GTA you are playing.

## Quick comparison: what cheats affect in each game

| Game | Can you save after cheating? | Achievements/trophies affected? | Can cheats get stuck in your save? |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA 5 | Yes | Disabled for that session | Effects generally do not persist after reloading |
| GTA IV | Yes | Disabled for that session, with a warning | Cheat use is counted in stats |
| San Andreas (original) | Yes | No achievements on original PS2 | Yes, several cheats can persist |
| San Andreas (Definitive Edition) | Yes | Disabled when cheats are used | Yes, same risk as the original |
| Vice City | Yes | Disabled in the Definitive Edition | Some cheat effects can persist |
| GTA III | Yes | Disabled in the Definitive Edition | Some cheat effects can persist |

Versions differ. Mobile ports, the Xbox 360 San Andreas port, PS2 Classics and the Definitive Edition do not all behave identically, so treat this as the general rule and test on a backup if you are unsure.

## GTA 5: achievements off, saves fine

In GTA 5, **using any cheat disables trophies and achievements for the rest of that play session.** The game tells you when you activate a cheat. You can keep playing, keep saving and keep finishing missions, but nothing will pop until you reload.

What you should know:

- **Saving works normally.** Your save file is not marked or damaged.
- **Cheat effects do not carry over** in any meaningful way. Invincibility times out, spawned vehicles are not stored like owned cars, and weather resets.
- **Getting achievements back** is simple: load a save or restart the game and play without cheating.
- **Some missions block cheats**, so you cannot accidentally break a scripted sequence.

## GTA San Andreas: the one to be careful with

San Andreas is the game where "do cheats ruin saves?" actually matters. Saving with cheats is allowed, but **some cheats change things that get written into your save permanently.** Others change the world state in ways that can confuse missions.

### Cheats that permanently change CJ

| Cheat | PC code | What gets saved |
|---|---|---|
| Max Muscle | BUFFMEUP | CJ's muscle stat set to maximum |
| Max Fat | BTCDBCB | CJ's fat stat set to maximum |
| Skinny CJ | KVGYZQK | Removes fat and muscle |
| Hitman Level for All Weapons | PROFESSIONALKILLER | Max weapon skill for every weapon |

These are stat changes, not timed effects. Once you save, they are part of your game. You can reverse body changes the normal way (gym or food) or with the opposite cheat, but weapon skills do not drop back down. That might be what you want. Just know it is a one-way door.

### Cheats that change the world

| Cheat | PC code | Why it is risky |
|---|---|---|
| Always Midnight | XJVSNAJ | Freezes the clock at midnight |
| Orange Sky (21:00) | OFVIAC | Freezes the clock at 21:00 |
| Pedestrians Riot | STATEOFEMERGENCY | Everyone fights everyone |
| Pedestrians Attack You | BAGOWPG | Every pedestrian hunts you |
| Gang Members Everywhere | MROEMZH | Gangs take over the streets |

The time-freeze cheats are the infamous ones. San Andreas has missions and activities that only open at certain hours, plus timed events that assume the clock is moving. **Saving while the clock is frozen is widely reported to cause problems**, ranging from missions that never become available to a clock that stays stuck after loading. Entering the cheat again may toggle it off, but players report mixed results across versions, so do not rely on it.

The pedestrian cheats can also be saved in some versions, which makes escort missions and anything with civilians around much harder.

### Achievements in the Definitive Edition

The original PS2, Xbox and PC releases had no achievements. **The Definitive Edition does, and using cheats disables them.** Exactly how long the lockout lasts (until you reload, or tied to the save) is something players report differently across platforms and patches, so the safest approach is simple: keep a clean save for achievement hunting.

## GTA Vice City: mostly safe, a few gotchas

Vice City's cheats are mostly temporary: health, weapons, vehicle spawns and weather. A few deserve caution:

- **Pedestrian behavior cheats** like `FIGHTFIGHTFIGHT` (riot) and `NOBODYLIKESME` (everyone attacks you) can make missions chaotic, and some players report the effect sticking after saving.
- **Traffic and world cheats** that change car colors or driving behavior are usually harmless but can be confusing if they carry over.
- **Blow up all cars** (`BIGBANG`) can destroy mission-critical vehicles if you use it at the wrong moment.

In the Definitive Edition, cheats disable trophies and achievements, the same as San Andreas. Every code is on the [GTA Vice City cheats hub](/gta-vice-city-cheats/).

## GTA III: simple and mostly harmless

GTA III has the smallest cheat list of the trilogy, and most of it is temporary. The ones to watch are the **pedestrian cheats** (`ITSALLGOINGMAAAD` riot and `NOBODYLIKESME` attack) and **BANGBANGBANG**, which blows up every nearby car, including any you need for a mission.

GTA III also has the series' only straight cash cheat, `IFIWEREARICHMAN`, which adds $250,000. Money is saved like any other money, so it stays in your bank once you save. In the Definitive Edition, using cheats disables trophies and achievements.

## GTA IV: warned, counted, but still saveable

GTA IV handles cheats more transparently than any other game in the series. Every cheat is a **phone number** dialed on Niko's phone. When you use one:

- **The game warns you** that cheating affects achievements.
- **Achievements are disabled** for that session.
- **Cheat use is recorded** in the in-game stats.
- **You can still save.** The save itself is not locked or corrupted.

Because GTA IV cheats are mostly instant effects (health, weapons, vehicle spawns, wanted level), there is little that can get stuck in your save. The [GTA 4 cheats hub](/gta-4-cheats/) lists every number, including both episodes.

## How to back up your save before cheating

Whatever game you are playing, this habit costs you ten seconds and can save you hours.

1. **Save your progress normally** in your usual slot before you touch a cheat.
2. **Make a second save in a different slot.** This is your cheat save.
3. **Only save cheated progress to the cheat slot.** Never overwrite your clean slot after cheating.
4. **On PC, copy your save folder** to another location for an extra layer of safety. On consoles, use cloud saves or copy to a USB drive where your console supports it.
5. **Name or note which slot is which.** It is easy to forget a week later.
6. **Before an achievement or 100% run, reload the clean slot** and restart the game so nothing carries over.

## The bottom line

Cheats never lock you out of saving in GTA. In GTA 5 and GTA IV, the cost is losing achievements for that session, and the save itself stays clean. In San Andreas, and to a lesser extent Vice City and GTA III, a handful of cheats can bake themselves into your save, with time-freeze and riot cheats being the classic troublemakers. Keep a clean backup slot and you can cheat freely without risking your playthrough.
