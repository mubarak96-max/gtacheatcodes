## Is there a money cheat in GTA Vice City?

No. GTA Vice City has no money cheat on PlayStation 2, Xbox, PC or the 2021 Definitive Edition. It is the one game in the original 3D trilogy that never got one: GTA III before it has IFIWEREARICHMAN, and San Andreas after it has HESOYAM, each adding $250,000. Type either into Vice City and nothing happens.

So if you want Tommy rolling in cash, you have to earn it. The good news: Vice City is built around exactly that, and once your business empire is running, money stops being a problem. This guide covers why the cheat does not exist, which cheats save you money anyway, and the fastest legitimate ways to get rich.

## Why there is no Vice City money cheat

Rockstar has never officially explained it, so we will not pretend to know the reason. What we can say is that money in Vice City is tied closely to the story. Tommy's whole arc is about taking over the city's businesses, and buying property is a core part of the game's progression. A cheat that handed you unlimited cash would short-circuit that loop more than in GTA III, where money mostly buys guns. Whatever the reasoning, the result is the same: no code exists, and any website or video claiming to have a "secret" Vice City money cheat is wrong.

## How to make money in Vice City: assets

The biggest money-maker in Vice City is property. Once Tommy has enough cash and the story opens up, properties across the city are marked for sale. Buying one is step one. Most business properties then have their own set of missions; complete them and the property becomes an **asset** that generates income on its own.

### How assets work

1. **Buy the property.** Walk into the for-sale marker and pay the price.
2. **Complete its missions.** Each business has its own short mission chain.
3. **Collect the income.** Once the missions are done, the business earns money over time. Cash builds up at a pickup at the property, up to a maximum, so visit regularly to collect it.

This is passive income: it keeps adding up while you do other things. The earlier you set up your businesses, the more they pay you over the rest of the game. The story also expects you to build up this business empire before the final missions open, so you are going to do it anyway.

### Which assets first?

Prioritise businesses whose missions you can finish quickly and that unlock story progress. The Malibu Club and the Print Works are central to Tommy's rise, so most players set those up early. After that, pick whichever mission chains you enjoy; the car showroom chain at Sunshine Autos is a favourite for players who like stealing cars.

## Side jobs that pay cash

Side missions pay money for each level you complete, with the payout increasing as you go. Several also unlock permanent perks once you finish enough levels.

| Side job | How to start | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Vigilante | Get in a police vehicle and start the mission | Cash per level; finishing raises Tommy's armor cap |
| Paramedic | Get in an ambulance | Cash per level; finishing gives infinite sprint |
| Firefighter | Get in a fire truck | Cash per level; finishing makes Tommy fireproof |
| Taxi Driver | Get in a taxi | Cash per fare; completing 100 fares upgrades taxis with a hop ability |
| Pizza Boy | Get on a pizza delivery bike | Cash per delivery; finishing raises Tommy's health cap |

Paramedic is the one to do early regardless of money, because infinite sprint makes the rest of the game much less tedious. Vigilante is a good earner if you are comfortable with firefights.

## Other quick ways to make cash

- **Rob stores.** Many shops in Vice City can be held up: aim a gun at the clerk and cash starts dropping. It earns money fast but also gives you a wanted level, so have an escape (or LEAVEMEALONE) ready.
- **Rampages.** Rampage icons scattered around the city start timed challenges, and completing one pays a cash reward.
- **Unique Stunt Jumps.** Clearing a unique stunt jump pays a cash bonus. A fast car helps; the Hotring Racer and Sabre Turbo spawns are ideal.
- **Races and story missions.** Story missions are the backbone of your income in the early game, and several side races pay prize money too.

## What does not make money

### Hidden packages

Vice City has 100 hidden packages tucked around the map. They are worth collecting, but not for cash: every ten you find unlocks a new weapon pickup at your safehouses. That saves you trips to Ammu-Nation, but they will not fill your wallet. Treat them as an arsenal upgrade, not a money source.

### Dying and getting busted

Getting wasted or busted costs you. You lose your weapons and some cash. If you are trying to save up for a property, play it safe, and use ASPIRINE and LEAVEMEALONE liberally. Those are cheats that pay for themselves.

## What about trainers and mods?

On PC, third-party trainers and save editors can change Tommy's money directly. They are not cheat codes; they modify the game, can break saves and are not supported by Rockstar. If you go that way, back up your save first. Our [cheats vs mods vs trainers](/guides/gta-cheats-vs-mods-vs-trainers/) guide explains the difference and the risks. On consoles, there is no such option.
