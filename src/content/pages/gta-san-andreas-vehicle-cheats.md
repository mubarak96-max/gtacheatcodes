## What vehicle cheats are in GTA San Andreas?

GTA San Andreas vehicle cheats spawn a ride right next to CJ, from a Rhino tank and a Hydra fighter jet to a golf Caddy and a hearse. There are also traffic cheats that change every car on the road: flying cars, flying boats, nitro for everything and perfect handling. Between them, you can turn San Andreas into a military sandbox or a cartoon in about ten seconds.

The interactive table above is filtered to vehicle spawns. Below, we go through each one with what it's actually good for, then cover the traffic cheats.

## How to spawn a vehicle in San Andreas

1. Go **outdoors** and find some open space. Vehicles spawn beside CJ and need room.
2. Get into normal gameplay, not paused.
3. **PC:** type the code. **Console:** press the sequence with the d-pad for directions.
4. Look for "Cheat activated" and the vehicle appears.

Spawning a big vehicle like a tanker or Hydra in a cramped alley can fail or drop it somewhere awkward. Spawn cheats may also be restricted inside some interiors or during certain missions.

### Rhino tank (AIWPRTON)

The Rhino is the toughest thing on the ground in San Andreas. It's slow, but it shrugs off gunfire and flattens traffic. Pair it with `BRINGITON` (six stars) for a classic last stand, or `AEZAKMI` (never wanted) if you just want to cruise around in a tank.

### Hydra (JUMPJET)

The Hydra is a vertical take-off fighter jet, so you can lift off from almost any open street. It's the fastest way to get from Los Santos to Las Venturas. It also carries missiles, which makes it brutal in a dogfight. The hover mode takes practice; take off straight up, then transition to forward flight.

### Hunter (OHDUDE)

The Hunter is an attack helicopter. It's slower than the Hydra but far easier to control at low speed, which makes it better for precise flying, landing on rooftops and attacking ground targets.

### Stuntplane (URKQSRK)

A small, nimble prop plane. It's weaponless, so use it for loops, rolls and flying under bridges rather than combat.

### Jetpack and parachute

The jetpack is so popular it gets its own page: see our [San Andreas jetpack cheat guide](/guides/gta-san-andreas-jetpack-cheat/). The parachute (`AIYPWZQP`) is its natural partner. Climb high in a Hydra or Hunter, bail out and float down.

### Flying cars (RIPAZHA)

Flying cars is one of San Andreas's signature cheats. Accelerate hard and pull back to lift off. It's clumsy at first, but once you get the feel you can hop rivers or soar over the city in a Hotring Racer.

### Perfect handling and nitro

`PGGOMOY` makes cars stick to the road, and on some versions lets them jump using the horn. `COXEFGU` gives every car nitrous. Together they turn even a junky sedan into a rally car. Taxi drivers can use `VKYPQCF`, which gives taxis nitro and a hop on L3.
