## How do GTA 5 cheats work on Xbox?

GTA 5 cheats on Xbox are button combos you enter in Story Mode using A, B, X, Y, the bumpers (LB, RB), the triggers (LT, RT) and the d-pad. Xbox 360, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S all use exactly the same combos. Xbox One and Series X|S also support the 1-999 phone cheats, which arrived with the 2014 Xbox One version.

Every code on this page is written in Xbox notation, so you never have to translate a PlayStation guide in your head. That translation, by the way, is where most Xbox players go wrong. More on that below.

## GTA 5 on Xbox 360 vs Xbox One vs Series X|S

| Version | Released | Button combos | Phone cheats | Returning-player vehicles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Xbox 360 | 2013 | Yes | No | No |
| Xbox One | 2014 | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Xbox Series X\|S | 2022 | Yes | Yes | Yes |

The Xbox 360 version uses the same button combos as every later release but doesn't have phone cheats. The Duke O'Death, Kraken and Dodo, the three returning-player vehicles, only exist on Xbox One and newer, and they have no button combo, so you must dial them on the phone. The Series X|S version, released in March 2022 alongside the PS5 version, didn't change any cheats.

One thing to check: if you're playing the Xbox One version through backward compatibility on a Series X|S, the cheats are still the same. You just don't get the native Series X|S version's graphical upgrades.

## Why do Xbox players get GTA 5 cheats wrong?

Because half the internet writes GTA cheats in PlayStation buttons, and the Xbox **X** button is not PlayStation's Cross. Here is the full translation:

| PlayStation | Xbox |
|---|---|
| Cross | A |
| Circle | B |
| Square | X |
| Triangle | Y |
| L1 | LB |
| R1 | RB |
| L2 | LT |
| R2 | RT |

The trap is obvious once you see it: PlayStation **Cross** looks like an X, but it's Xbox **A**. PlayStation **Square** sits where Xbox **X** sits. If you follow a PlayStation guide and press Xbox X every time you see "X", every cheat with Cross or Square in it will fail. Stick to Xbox-notation lists like the ones on this page.

The same layout logic holds on PlayStation's side, too. If you play on both systems, keep the [PS4 and PS5 version of this guide](/guides/gta-5-cheats-ps4-ps5/) handy.

## Tips for entering cheats on an Xbox controller

- **Pull triggers fully.** LT and RT are analog. A light squeeze may not register as a press. Every combo with a trigger in it needs a clean, full pull.
- **Use the d-pad, not the stick.** Directions in GTA 5 cheats are d-pad only.
- **Watch the Elite paddles.** If you use an Xbox Elite or a controller with back paddles mapped to face buttons, double-check what each paddle sends. Paddles are fine to use if they're mapped correctly.
- **Check Xbox Accessories remaps.** Button remaps set in the Xbox Accessories app apply to the game. If A is remapped, every code with A fails.
- **Steady rhythm.** Go quick, but don't mash. Missed presses break the whole combo.

## Do GTA 5 cheats disable achievements on Xbox?

Yes. Entering any cheat disables achievements for the rest of that play session on Xbox 360, Xbox One and Series X|S. It isn't permanent. Reload a save or restart without cheating and achievements unlock normally again.
