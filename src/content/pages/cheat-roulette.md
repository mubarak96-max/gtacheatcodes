## How to use the GTA Cheat Roulette

The GTA Cheat Roulette turns cheat codes into a game of chance. Instead of reaching for the same three cheats every time, you let the reel decide. It's a random cheat generator for GTA 5, San Andreas, Vice City, GTA IV and GTA III, and every spin comes with a small challenge to make the result actually mean something.

1. **Pick a game.** Choose the GTA you're playing. The roulette only spins from that game's cheat list, so the result will always be a real code for it.
2. **Spin.** The reel rolls and lands on one cheat.
3. **Read the result.** You get the cheat's name, what it does, and its code for your platform: a button combo, a PC code or a phone number.
4. **Take the mini challenge.** Each spin suggests a quick challenge built around that cheat, like surviving a chase or reaching a landmark.
5. **Enter the cheat and play.** Punch the code in, complete the challenge, then spin again.

If a combo is long and you keep fluffing it, take it to the [GTA Cheat Trainer](/tools/cheat-trainer/) for a few practice runs first. If you'd rather choose deliberately, the [GTA Cheat Finder](/tools/cheat-finder/) lists everything.

## Why spin a random GTA cheat?

Because most players use the same five cheats forever. Health, weapons, wanted level off, tank, done. Every GTA game hides weirder stuff in its cheat list: Elvis impersonators, pink traffic, moon gravity, cars that float away when you hit them. The roulette forces you into that weird stuff, and that's where the best stories come from.

It's also a great fix for "I've finished the story, now what?" A random cheat plus a challenge gives you a fresh goal in a map you already know by heart.

## Chaos challenge ideas

The roulette gives you a mini challenge on every spin, but here are bigger formats to build a whole session around.

### The escalation run

Spin once every five minutes and **stack every cheat you land**. Never turn anything off. By the half-hour mark in San Andreas you might be fat, flying a car through a sandstorm while Elvis impersonators riot below. Survive as long as you can without using a health cheat the roulette didn't give you.

### Mission roulette

Before every story mission, spin and keep the result active for the whole mission. Rhino tank before a stealth mission? Good luck. Pink traffic before a car chase? Every car you could jack on the way is now pink. This works best on a fresh playthrough save you don't mind experimenting with.

### Reach the landmark

Spin, enter the cheat, then race to a famous spot: Mount Chiliad in GTA 5 or San Andreas, the top of a skyscraper, the airport. The cheat decides how you get there. San Andreas's Mega Jump or GTA 5's Super Jump might make it easy; GTA 5's Drunk Mode or Slidey Cars won't.

### Survive the stars

Raise your wanted level to the maximum, then spin. You must survive two minutes with whatever the reel gives you. In San Andreas that might be the Six-Star Wanted Level cheat stacking on top, or the Hydra jet saving your life. In GTA 5, landing Lower Wanted Level is basically a get-out-of-jail card, so add a rule that it doesn't count.

### The no-repeat game

Spin and use each cheat once. If the roulette lands on a cheat you've already used, you lose a life. Three lives and you're out. It's a neat way to work through an entire game's cheat list.

## Streamer and party game rules

The roulette works brilliantly as a group or stream format because everyone can see the reel and react. Here are rule sets that work.

### Stream rules

- **Chat spins.** Every time a viewer hits a milestone (a follow, a sub or whatever your channel uses), you spin. The cheat goes on immediately, mid-mission or not.
- **Timed chaos.** Spin every ten minutes on a visible timer. It builds anticipation and gives clip-worthy moments.
- **Veto tokens.** Give chat one veto per hour. If the reel lands on something boring like a weather cheat, chat can force a re-spin.
- **Penalty box.** Fail the mini challenge and take a penalty: no vehicles for five minutes, drive only in first person, or play with the Pedestrians Attack You cheat on.

### Couch party rules

- **Pass the pad.** Each player spins, enters the cheat themselves (on a real controller, no help from the screen), and has three minutes to finish the challenge. Points for each success.
- **Fumble rule.** If you mess up the combo entry, you drink your soda, do a forfeit, or hand the controller over. Practising in the [GTA Cheat Trainer](/tools/cheat-trainer/) beforehand is allowed, and encouraged.
- **Trilogy tournament.** Each round uses a different game: GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, then GTA 5. Highest score across the four rounds wins.
- **Funniest moment vote.** At the end of the night, everyone votes for the best chaos moment. Our [funny GTA cheats](/guides/funny-gta-cheats/) list has inspiration for which spins produce the biggest laughs.

## Safe-save advice before you start spinning

Random cheats are fun precisely because you don't control them, which means some spins will land on cheats that can cause trouble if you save the game afterwards. Protect your progress:

1. **Use a dedicated chaos save.** Before you start, save your real progress to one slot, then make a copy on a different slot for roulette sessions. Only ever play roulette from the copy.
2. **Don't save after world-altering cheats.** In San Andreas, the cheats most likely to cause long-term trouble are the ones that change how the world runs: Always Midnight and Orange Sky (time freezes), Gang Members Everywhere, Pedestrians Riot and Pedestrians Attack You. Blow Up All Cars can also wreck mission vehicles. Reload instead of saving. Our guide to [San Andreas cheats that ruin your save](/guides/gta-san-andreas-cheats-that-ruin-your-save/) has the full breakdown.
3. **Mind GTA 5's trophies and achievements.** Entering any cheat in GTA 5 disables trophies and achievements for that play session. If you're hunting a specific trophy, finish it first, then spin. Details are in our [GTA 5 cheats and trophies guide](/guides/gta-5-cheats-trophies-achievements/).
4. **Watch the autosave.** GTA 5 autosaves after missions. If you've been running chaos cheats and you care about your main save, check your autosave settings or use a separate profile slot.
5. **Reload to reset.** Most cheats are cleared when you load a save. When a session gets too chaotic, just reload and start fresh.
