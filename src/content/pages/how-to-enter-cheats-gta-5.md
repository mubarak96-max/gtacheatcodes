## How do you enter cheats in GTA 5?

You enter cheats in GTA 5 in one of three ways: a button combo on a PlayStation or Xbox controller, a word typed into the PC cheat console, or a 1-999 number dialed on the in-game phone. All three methods trigger the same effects, and all three only work in Story Mode during live gameplay. The rest of this guide walks through each method step by step, then covers the part nobody else explains properly: what to do when a cheat just won't fire.

Before you start, a quick reality check that saves headaches:

- **Story Mode only.** Cheats do nothing in GTA Online.
- **Trophies and achievements switch off** for the session once you use any cheat.
- **There is no money cheat**, on any platform, no matter what a video thumbnail says.

Every code is listed on the main [GTA 5 cheats](/gta-5-cheats/) hub. This page is about technique.

## How do you enter cheats in GTA 5 on PS4 and PS5?

PlayStation cheats are button combos using the face buttons (Cross, Circle, Triangle, Square), the shoulder buttons (L1, R1, L2, R2) and the d-pad.

1. **Load into Story Mode** and get to normal gameplay. Pause menus, the map screen and cutscenes don't accept cheats.
2. **Read the full sequence once** so you're not checking a guide mid-combo.
3. **Enter it quickly and in one go.** There's no strict published time limit, but hesitating for a few seconds can reset the input.
4. **Use the d-pad for directions.** Left means d-pad Left, not a flick of the left stick.
5. **Watch for confirmation.** A small "cheat activated" style notice appears when it works.

Example: Max Health & Armor on PlayStation is **Circle, L1, Triangle, R2, Cross, Square, Circle, Right, Square, L1, L1, L1**.

On PS5, nothing changes. The DualSense has the same layout as the DualShock 4, and the PS5 version of GTA 5 uses identical codes. The full PlayStation-notation table lives in [GTA 5 cheats for PS4 and PS5](/guides/gta-5-cheats-ps4-ps5/).

## How do you enter cheats in GTA 5 on Xbox One and Series X|S?

Same idea, different labels. Xbox uses A, B, X, Y, the bumpers (LB, RB), the triggers (LT, RT) and the d-pad.

1. **Load Story Mode** and get into free roam or normal mission gameplay.
2. **Enter the full combo quickly** with no long pauses.
3. **Directions are d-pad only.** The left stick won't register.
4. **Pull the triggers all the way.** A half-pressed LT or RT is the classic Xbox misfire.
5. **Look for the confirmation** at the top of the screen.

Example: Max Health & Armor on Xbox is **B, LB, Y, RT, A, X, B, Right, X, LB, LB, LB**.

Xbox 360, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S all use the same combos. See [GTA 5 cheats for Xbox](/guides/gta-5-cheats-xbox/) for the complete Xbox-notation table.

## How do you enter cheats in GTA 5 on PC?

PC players get the easiest method: a cheat console where you type the cheat's name.

1. **Load into Story Mode.**
2. **Press the tilde key (`~`).** On a US keyboard it's the key under Esc, left of 1. On other layouts it's usually the same physical key, but the printed symbol differs. If tilde doesn't work, try that top-left key anyway.
3. **Type the cheat word**, for example `TURTLE` or `BUZZOFF`. Capitals are optional.
4. **Press Enter.**

If your keyboard layout makes that key awkward, remember that controller combos and phone numbers work on PC too. You can plug in a gamepad and use the Xbox codes, or open the phone with the Up arrow. Both the Enhanced and Legacy PC editions use the same console words. For PC-specific quirks, read the [GTA 5 PC cheats](/guides/gta-5-pc-cheats/) guide.

## How do you use phone cheats in GTA 5?

The phone method works on PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S and PC.

1. **Bring up the phone:** d-pad Up on a controller, Up arrow on PC.
2. **Open the Contacts app** and use the on-screen prompt to bring up the keypad.
3. **Dial the full number**, including 1-999. Example: 1-999-887-853 for Max Health & Armor.
4. **Press call.** The cheat activates straight away.

The phone has no timing window, which makes it the most forgiving method on console. It's also the only console route to the three returning-player vehicles, which have no button combo. Every number is listed in [GTA 5 phone cheats](/guides/gta-5-phone-cheats/).

## Why isn't my GTA 5 cheat working?

If a cheat isn't activating, work through this list in order. In our experience the first three explain nearly every failure.

### 1. You were too slow (or too fast in the wrong way)

Combos need to go in briskly. Long pauses between inputs can reset the sequence, so you end up entering the second half of the code into nothing. Equally, mashing so fast that a press doesn't register will break it. Aim for a steady, deliberate rhythm. If you keep failing a long combo, practice it in the [cheat trainer](/tools/cheat-trainer/), which times your inputs.

### 2. You used the stick instead of the d-pad

Directions in GTA 5 cheats always mean the d-pad. Analog stick movement isn't read as a cheat input. This catches out a lot of people on controllers where the d-pad is awkward to reach.

### 3. One button was wrong

Long combos like Skyfall run to sixteen inputs. A single Square where it should be Circle and nothing happens. Check the PlayStation-to-Xbox translation especially: PlayStation Square is Xbox **X**, and PlayStation Cross is Xbox **A**. Mixing those up is the single most common cross-platform mistake.

### 4. A mission is blocking cheats

Some story missions disable cheats entirely, and others ignore specific effects. Wanted-level cheats may not clear a star count the mission has scripted. If a code works in free roam but not in a mission, that's the mission, not your thumbs.

### 5. The context is wrong

Some cheats only make sense in certain situations:

- **Lower Wanted Level** does nothing if you have no stars.
- **Vehicle spawns** need open space. Garages, interiors and narrow alleys can cause problems.
- **Skyfall and Give Parachute** work best on foot in the open world.
- **Toggles toggle.** If you enter Moon Gravity twice, you've turned it on and back off.

### 6. You're in GTA Online

Cheats don't work in GTA Online at all. If you loaded into Online by accident, switch back to Story Mode from the pause menu.

### 7. You're on PC and the console won't open

Check the keyboard layout first. If the top-left key under Esc does nothing, try another layout in Windows, or skip the console and use the phone or a controller. Overlays from other apps can occasionally grab that key, so close them and try again.

## Does the PC Enhanced vs Legacy edition change anything?

Not for cheats. Rockstar released an Enhanced edition of GTA 5 for PC that sits alongside the older Legacy edition. Both read the same console words, the same phone numbers and the same controller combos. If you're switching between them, your cheat knowledge carries over. Your save situation and mods are a separate topic, so check before moving saves across.

## Final tips before you start cheating

- **Save first.** Cheats don't persist, but trophies and achievements are off for the session once you use one. A clean save gives you a fresh start.
- **Pick the right method for the moment.** Phone for repeats, combos while driving, console on PC.
- **Learn five codes properly** rather than half-remembering thirty. Health, weapons, lower wanted, a vehicle and one fun cheat will cover most sessions.
- **Keep it single-player.** Cheating in GTA Online with third-party tools risks a ban and ruins it for others.
