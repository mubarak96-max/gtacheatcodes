## Why funny GTA cheats are the best GTA cheats

Funny GTA cheats are the codes that don't help you win at all. They just make the game ridiculous. Rockstar has packed every GTA since GTA III with them: cheats that fill the streets with Elvis impersonators, turn gravity down to moon levels or make every car on the road pink. This list ranks the silliest cheats across GTA 5, San Andreas, Vice City and GTA III, with the codes and our best tips for squeezing even more laughs out of each one.

Xbox and Switch inputs are translated from the PlayStation combo: Cross = A (Xbox) / B (Switch), Circle = B / A, Square = X / Y, Triangle = Y / X, L1/R1 = LB/RB or L/R, L2/R2 = LT/RT or ZL/ZR. Switch applies to the Definitive Edition versions of GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas. For every code on every platform, search the [GTA Cheat Finder](/tools/cheat-finder/).

## The funniest GTA 5 cheats

### Moon Gravity (FLOATER)

Gravity drops to moon levels. Cars bounce off each other and drift away, ragdolling pedestrians hang in the air, and a simple fender-bender becomes a slow-motion art piece.

| Platform | Code |
|---|---|
| PlayStation | Left, Left, L1, R1, L1, Right, Left, L1, Left |
| Xbox | Left, Left, LB, RB, LB, Right, Left, LB, Left |
| PC console | FLOATER |
| Phone | 1-999-356-2837 |

**Make it funnier:** add **Slidey Cars** (below) and try to park anything. Or drive off the top of a parking garage and watch your car float down like a leaf.

### Drunk Mode (LIQUOR)

Your character stumbles, sways and staggers as if they've had far too many. Walking in a straight line becomes a challenge. Trevor fits the part a little too well.

| Platform | Code |

| Xbox | Y, Right, Right, Left, Right, X, B, Left |
| PC console | LIQUOR |
| Phone | 1-999-547-861 |

**Make it funnier:** try to complete a foot chase, or enter Slow Motion (SLOWMO) on top for a woozy, dreamlike stagger.

Drops you from high in the sky over Los Santos. It's half spectacle, half panic. There's no parachute unless you bring one.

Tyre grip disappears and every car handles like it's on ice. Every corner becomes a drift, mostly into a lamppost.

## The funniest San Andreas cheats

San Andreas is the silliest game in the series. It has more theme cheats, more body cheats and more traffic cheats than anything before or since.

### Elvis Is Everywhere

Pedestrians are replaced with Elvis impersonators. Whole crowds of them, waiting at bus stops and crossing the road.

- **PlayStation:** L1, Circle, Triangle, L1, L1, Square, L2, Up, Down, Left
- **Xbox:** LB, B, Y, LB, LB, X, LT, Up, Down, Left
- **Switch:** L, A, X, L, L, Y, ZL, Up, Down, Left
- **PC:** BLUESUEDESHOES (or ASBHGRB)

**Make it funnier:** add **Pedestrians Riot** (PC: STATEOFEMERGENCY) and watch an all-Elvis street brawl. Just don't save afterwards; riot cheats can mess with missions.

### Beach Party Theme

Pedestrians switch to swimsuits and beach vehicles start appearing. It's spring break, everywhere, all the time, including the desert.

- **PlayStation:** Up, Up, Down, Down, Square, Circle, L1, R1, Triangle, Down
- **Xbox:** Up, Up, Down, Down, X, B, LB, RB, Y, Down
- **Switch:** Up, Up, Down, Down, Y, A, L, R, X, Down
- **PC:** CIKGCGX

**Make it funnier:** add the **Sandstorm** cheat (CWJXUOC) for the worst beach day in history.

### Pink Traffic

Every car on the road is pink. It's the same combo in Vice City (PC: AHAIRDRESSERSCAR).

- **PlayStation:** Circle, L1, Down, L2, Left, Cross, R1, L1, Right, Circle
- **Xbox:** B, LB, Down, LT, Left, A, RB, LB, Right, B
- **Switch:** A, L, Down, ZL, Left, B, R, L, Right, A
- **PC:** LLQPFBN

**Make it funnier:** add **Aggressive Traffic** (YLTEICZ) so a pink army is trying to ram you.

### Cars Float Away When Hit

Bump any car and it gently drifts off into the sky. Rear-ending someone at a red light becomes a balloon launch.

- **PlayStation:** Square, R2, Down, Down, Left, Down, Left, Left, L2, Cross
- **Xbox:** X, RT, Down, Down, Left, Down, Left, Left, LT, A
- **Switch:** Y, ZR, Down, Down, Left, Down, Left, Left, ZL, B
- **PC:** BSXSGGC

**Make it funnier:** spawn a Rhino (AIWPRTON) or a Dozer (EEGCYXT) and clear a freeway like a leaf blower.

### Max Fat CJ and the Huge Bunny Hop

Max Fat turns CJ instantly obese, and the Huge Bunny Hop lets bicycles leap absurd heights. Together they make a very large man on a very small bike, jumping over houses.

- **Max Fat (PlayStation):** Triangle, Up, Up, Left, Right, Square, Circle, Down · **PC:** BTCDBCB
- **Huge Bunny Hop (PlayStation):** Triangle, Square, Circle, Circle, Square, Circle, Circle, L1, L2, L2, R1, R2 · **PC:** CJPHONEHOME
- **Xbox:** Max Fat is Y, Up, Up, Left, Right, X, B, Down; Huge Bunny Hop is Y, X, B, B, X, B, B, LB, LT, LT, RB, RT

**Make it funnier:** add **Mega Jump** (KANGAROO) so even off the bike, Big CJ leaps like a kangaroo.

### Flying Cars and Flying Boats

San Andreas's flying cars (PC: RIPAZHA; PlayStation: Square, Down, L2, Up, L1, Circle, Up, Cross, Left) let you take off by accelerating and pulling back. Flying Boats (PC: AFSNMSMW) does the same for anything that floats. A Caddy at cruising altitude is a sight to behold.

## The funniest Vice City and GTA III cheats

### Cars Drive on Water (Vice City)

Every car drives across the sea like it's tarmac. Race a Caddy across the bay.

- **PlayStation:** Right, R2, Circle, R1, L2, Square, R1, R2
- **Xbox:** Right, RT, B, RB, LT, X, RB, RT
- **PC:** SEAWAYS

### Flying Cars (GTA III and Vice City)

The same combo in both games: Right, R2, Circle, R1, L2, Down, L1, R1 on PlayStation (Right, RT, B, RB, LT, Down, LB, RB on Xbox). PC is CHITTYCHITTYBB in GTA III and COMEFLYWITHME in Vice City.

### Invisible Cars, Wheels Only (GTA III and Vice City)

Car bodies vanish and only the wheels remain, so traffic becomes a parade of spinning tyres with floating drivers.

- **GTA III (PlayStation):** L1, L1, Square, R2, Triangle, L1, Triangle · **PC:** ANICESETOFWHEELS
- **Vice City (PlayStation):** Triangle, L1, Triangle, R2, Square, L1, L1 · **PC:** WHEELSAREALLINEED

### Fat Tommy and Tommy Smokes a Cigarette (Vice City)

Fat Tommy (PC: DEEPFRIEDMARSBARS; PlayStation: Triangle, Up, Up, Left, Right, Square, Circle, Down) gives Tommy a belly. CERTAINDEATH, a PC-only code, makes him light up a cigarette, and that's all it does. That's the joke.

### Play as Ken Rosenberg (Vice City, PC only)

Type MYSONISALAWYER to become Tommy's nervous lawyer. Rampaging as Ken never stops being funny.

## How to make GTA cheats even funnier

The real comedy comes from stacking. Most cheats can run at once, so combine cheats that affect different systems:

1. **Pick a world cheat:** Pink Traffic, Beach Party or Elvis Is Everywhere sets the scene.
2. **Add a physics cheat:** Moon Gravity, Flying Cars or Cars Float Away When Hit breaks the rules.
3. **Add a body or movement cheat:** Max Fat, Mega Jump, Drunk Mode or the Huge Bunny Hop changes how you move.
4. **Add a chaos trigger:** Pedestrians Riot or Aggressive Traffic lights the fuse.

## Keep the jokes off your main save

A few of these cheats can stick around or cause problems if you save afterwards, especially in San Andreas, where Pedestrians Riot, Pedestrians Attack You and Gang Members Everywhere are known troublemakers. Body cheats like Max Fat also change CJ's actual stats. Play on a spare save and read our guide to [San Andreas cheats that ruin your save](/guides/gta-san-andreas-cheats-that-ruin-your-save/).
