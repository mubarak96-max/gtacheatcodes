## Do GTA cheats work on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S?

**Yes, in every GTA you can actually play on them.** The catch is that "GTA on PS5" or "GTA on Series X" can mean three very different things: a native current-gen release (GTA 5), a remaster (the Definitive Edition trilogy), or an older version running through emulation or backward compatibility. Cheats work in all three, but the way you enter them, and which codes you use, depends on which kind you are running.

This guide sorts that out game by game, then points you to the right hub for the full code lists.

Storefront availability changes. Games get delisted, re-listed, added to subscriptions and pulled from them. Treat the table as a guide to how each version works, not a promise that you can buy it today.

## GTA 5 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

GTA 5's native current-gen versions launched in 2022 with better graphics, faster loading and performance modes. **The cheats are identical to every other console version.** If you knew the codes on PS3 or Xbox 360, they still work.

You have two ways to cheat:

1. **Button combos.** Enter the sequence quickly during gameplay. For example, invincibility is `R X R L R R1 R L X T` in our PlayStation notation (Right, Cross, Right, Left, Right, R1, Right, Left, Cross, Triangle on the d-pad and face buttons). On Xbox that becomes Right, A, Right, Left, Right, RB, Right, Left, A, Y.
2. **Phone numbers.** Bring up the phone and dial a 1-999 number, such as `1-999-724-654-5537` for invincibility. No combo memorizing, no timing.

A few current-gen specifics:

- **The DualSense works like any other pad.** The touchpad and adaptive triggers do not affect cheat input.
- **Returning-player bonus vehicles** (the Duke O'Death, Kraken submarine and Dodo seaplane) are tied to the enhanced versions of GTA 5 and may need a linked account. They have phone numbers but no button combo.
- **Cheats disable trophies and achievements** for the session, same as on older consoles.
- **Story-mode only.** None of this works in GTA Online.

## The Definitive Edition trilogy on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

*Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy - The Definitive Edition* remastered GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas in 2021, with native versions for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. **The classic cheat codes are still there.** They use the same button sequences as the PS2 originals, with buttons mapped to modern controllers.

| PlayStation token | Xbox button |
|---|---|
| Cross (X) | A |
| Circle (O) | B |
| Square (S) | X |
| Triangle (T) | Y |
| L1 / R1 | LB / RB |
| L2 / R2 | LT / RT |

So San Andreas's legendary `HESOYAM` (health, armor and $250,000) is `R1 R2 L1 X L D R U L D R U` on PS5, and RB, RT, LB, A, Left, Down, Right, Up, Left, Down, Right, Up on Xbox Series X|S.

Things worth knowing:

- **Enter combos during gameplay.** Not in the pause menu, and not during a cutscene.
- **Speed matters.** Like the originals, combos need to be entered fairly quickly and accurately. If one fails, take a breath and try again at a steady rhythm.
- **Trophies and achievements are disabled** when you use cheats. If you are going for completion, read our [guide to whether cheats disable saves](/guides/do-cheats-disable-saves-gta/) before you start.
- **Some cheats are permanent in your save** in San Andreas and Vice City, like CJ's body stats or riot modes. Save to a separate slot first.

## PS2 Classics on PS4 and PS5

Sony released several PS2-era GTA games as **PS2 Classics** on PS4: GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, Liberty City Stories and Vice City Stories. Because PS5 runs most PS4 software, these play on PS5 too.

These are the original games running in emulation, so **the original PS2 cheat codes work exactly as they did back then.** Same combos, same quirks, same "cheat activated" text.

Some practical notes:

- **Emulation can add input lag** compared with original hardware. If a long combo keeps failing, slow down slightly and press each button deliberately rather than mashing.
- **Original save quirks apply.** Cheats that permanently change your save on PS2 do the same here.
- **PS4 trophies were added** to these releases, and cheating may affect them. Check the trophy list before you cheat on a run you care about.
- **Availability varies.** Some of these listings have changed or disappeared over time. If you already own them, they stay in your library.

## Xbox backward compatibility

Microsoft's backward compatibility program lets Xbox Series X|S run selected Xbox 360 and original Xbox games. GTA's history on it is a bit messy:

- **GTA San Andreas** had an Xbox 360 version that was playable on newer Xboxes through backward compatibility. Its store availability has changed over the years, so check before buying.
- **Original Xbox versions** of GTA III and Vice City have had limited backward-compatibility support. Do not assume they are available.
- **GTA IV** and its episodes are backward compatible on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S if you own them.

In all cases, **you use the cheat codes for that original version**, mapped to your modern controller. The table above covers the button mapping.
