## Which platforms does GTA San Andreas have cheats on?

San Andreas launched on PlayStation 2 in 2004, then came to PC and the original Xbox in 2005. Since then it has been re-released many times. Cheat support depends on the version:

## How do you enter GTA San Andreas cheats on each platform?

The golden rule on every platform: enter the code **during gameplay**, not in the pause menu or the map screen. You can be on foot or in a vehicle. If it worked, the game shows a "Cheat activated" message in the top corner.

### PC

1. Load your save and get CJ moving in the world.
2. Type the code on your keyboard, for example `HESOYAM`.
3. Don't press Enter, and don't open anything first. There is no console.
4. Watch for "Cheat activated".

Typos are forgiving: if you mess up, just start typing the code again from the first letter. The full rundown is in our [San Andreas PC cheats guide](/guides/gta-san-andreas-pc-cheats/).

### PlayStation (PS2, PS4, PS5)

Press the sequence quickly, in order, using the d-pad for directions. Don't touch the analog sticks while entering a code, and don't pause. On PlayStation, `HESOYAM` is **R1, R2, L1, Cross, Left, Down, Right, Up, Left, Down, Right, Up**.

### Xbox

Xbox players use the same sequences translated to the Xbox pad: Cross becomes A, Circle becomes B, Square becomes X, Triangle becomes Y, L1/R1 become LB/RB and L2/R2 become LT/RT. So `HESOYAM` is **RB, RT, LB, A, Left, Down, Right, Up, Left, Down, Right, Up**.

### Nintendo Switch (Definitive Edition)

On Switch, Cross maps to B, Circle to A, Square to Y and Triangle to X, with L, R, ZL and ZR as the shoulders and triggers. `HESOYAM` becomes **R, ZR, L, B, Left, Down, Right, Up, Left, Down, Right, Up**. Use the d-pad buttons, not the left stick.

## Do cheats disable saving or achievements in San Andreas?

In the original releases, cheats don't stop you saving, though the game's stats menu keeps a record of cheat use. The Definitive Edition is stricter: using cheats can stop trophies or achievements from unlocking, so many players keep one clean save for unlocks and a second save for messing around. ## Quick tips for cheating in San Andreas without regret

1. **Make a backup save first.** Use a different save slot for your cheat sandbox.
2. **Enter codes in a quiet spot.** A missed button during a firefight wastes time.
3. **Re-enter a code if nothing happens.** Most failures are a skipped or doubled button.
4. **Clear your wanted level before saving.** It keeps your save tidy.
5. **Remember it's single player only.** San Andreas codes have nothing to do with GTA Online, and you should never try to cheat or mod there.
