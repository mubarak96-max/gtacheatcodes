## Are there GTA 6 cheats right now?

No. There are no confirmed GTA 6 cheats as of November 7, 2026. Grand Theft Auto VI has not launched yet, Rockstar has not published or teased any cheat codes, and nobody outside the studio has had the chance to find them. Any "GTA 6 cheats" list you see today, whether it is a video, a TikTok or a clickbait article, cannot have been tested on the real game.

What we can do is tell you exactly where things stand, what the history of the series suggests, and what to watch for once the game is out. This page will be updated as soon as real, verified codes appear.

This is an unofficial fan site with no connection to Rockstar Games or Take-Two. We will not post a GTA 6 code until it has been confirmed by players on the released game.

Here is what has been officially announced, as of early November 2026:

| Detail | Current status |

| Release date | Thursday, November 19, 2026 |
| Platforms | PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X\|S |

The road to that date was long. Rockstar released the first trailer in December 2023 with a 2025 release window. In May 2025 the game moved to May 26, 2026, and in November 2025 Rockstar pushed it again to November 19, 2026, saying the extra months were needed for polish. Take-Two has since repeated the November date in its investor updates, and pre-load details went out in early November 2026. Game Informer reported the pre-load download at around 212 GB, so clear some space.

GTA 5 came to PC about a year and a half after its console debut, so a later PC release for GTA 6 would fit the pattern, but Rockstar has not announced one.

## Has Rockstar confirmed cheats for GTA 6?

No. Rockstar has not mentioned cheat codes in its trailers, Newswire posts or store listings. That is normal. Rockstar has never announced cheats ahead of a GTA launch. Historically, codes surfaced after release, through official strategy guides, magazines, cheat websites, or players and dataminers digging into the game files.

So the silence tells us nothing either way. Expect clarity in the days and weeks after November 19.

## What GTA history suggests

Cheats are part of the series' DNA. Every mainline single-player Grand Theft Auto so far has shipped with some form of cheat system. The input method has changed with each generation:

| Era | Game | How you entered cheats |
|---|---|---|
| Top-down | GTA 1 and GTA 2 | Typing a special word as your player name |
| PS2 trilogy | GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas | Button combos on console; typed phrases on PC |
| HD Liberty City | GTA IV | Dialling phone numbers on Niko's in-game phone |
| Los Santos | GTA V | Button combos, a PC console box, and 1-999 phone numbers |

That evolution points in one direction: towards cheats that feel like they belong in the world. GTA IV hid them in the phone, and GTA V kept the button combos but added phone numbers such as 1-999-887-853 for health and armor (TURTLE on the PC console). ## Beware fake "GTA 6 cheats"

Search for GTA 6 cheats and you will find videos with confident thumbnails, "leaked" code lists and articles claiming to have the full set. They are not real. Here is how to spot them:

1. **They predate the game.** No one has played the retail game before launch, so no one can have tested a code.
2. **They recycle old codes.** Many lists are GTA 5 sequences with new names. If you see the exact GTA 5 tank or parachute combo, that is a giveaway.
3. **They promise money cheats.** GTA 5 had no money cheat at all. Claims of a "GTA 6 unlimited money code" are a classic bait.
4. **They link to downloads.** Any "cheat tool" or "mod menu" offered for an unreleased game is a malware risk. Do not install it.
5. **They mention GTA Online money.** Anything that promises free online cash is a scam or a ban waiting to happen. See our [GTA Online cheats guide](/guides/gta-online-cheats/) for why.

A good rule: if a source cannot tell you where a code came from, it did not come from the game.
