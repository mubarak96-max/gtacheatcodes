## How we ranked the best GTA cheats

The best GTA cheats of all time are the ones you actually come back to: codes that save a run, open up the map, or create the kind of chaos you tell friends about. We judged every cheat from GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and GTA 5 on three things:

- **Usefulness:** does it solve a real problem, like dying, being chased or being broke?
- **Fun:** does it make the game more entertaining to play?
- **Legacy:** is it the code everyone still remembers, decades later?

Each entry lists the code for every platform where it exists. Xbox and Switch inputs are translated from the PlayStation combo (Cross = A on Xbox and B on Switch, Circle = B / A, Square = X / Y, Triangle = Y / X, L1/R1 = LB/RB or L/R, L2/R2 = LT/RT or ZL/ZR). Switch codes apply to the Definitive Edition versions of GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas. If you want to search every code yourself, use the [GTA Cheat Finder](/tools/cheat-finder/).

## What makes a GTA cheat great?

The cheats at the top of this list share one trait: they remove friction without removing the game. HESOYAM keeps you in the action instead of hunting for health. The jetpack and Hydra let you see the map in new ways. AEZAKMI lets you experiment without the police ending every idea in thirty seconds.

The fun cheats, like Skyfall and Moon Gravity, earn their spots differently: they give you a reason to keep playing after the story is done. That's why the best way to use cheats is to mix the two. Use a utility cheat to stay alive, then a chaos cheat to make it interesting.
