## How to use the GTA Cheat Finder

The GTA Cheat Finder is one search box for every single-player cheat in GTA 5, GTA San Andreas, GTA Vice City, GTA IV and GTA III. Instead of flipping between five cheat lists, you type what you want and the finder pulls matching cheats from all five games at once, with the right buttons for your platform.

Here is the fastest way to get a code on screen:

1. **Type into the search box.** Search by cheat name ("Spawn Rhino Tank"), by effect ("tank", "jetpack", "gravity", "fog") or by the code itself ("HESOYAM", "PANZER", "PAINKILLER").
2. **Pick your platform.** Use the platform switch to choose PlayStation, Xbox, Switch, PC or Phone. Every combo in the list instantly rewrites itself for that controller.
3. **Narrow it down with a category.** Tap a category filter such as Health & Armor, Weapons & Combat, Wanted Level, Vehicle Spawns, Player & Skills, Weather & Time, Traffic & Driving, Pedestrians, or Chaos & Fun.
4. **Copy the code.** Hit the copy button to put the code or button sequence on your clipboard. That is handy for PC codes you want to paste into notes, or for sending a combo to a friend.
5. **Star your favourites.** Tap the favourite star on any cheat you use a lot. Favourites are saved in your browser, so your shortlist is there next time you open the page on the same device.
6. **Share a cheat.** Use the share option to send a specific cheat to someone else, which beats typing out twelve button presses in a group chat.

### What does the platform switch actually change?

The platform switch translates the same cheat into the inputs your machine expects. GTA cheat combos were designed on the PlayStation pad, and the other controllers map one-to-one onto it:

| PlayStation | Xbox | Switch (Definitive Edition) |
|---|---|---|
| Cross | A | B |
| Circle | B | A |
| Square | X | Y |
| Triangle | Y | X |
| L1 / R1 | LB / RB | L / R |
| L2 / R2 | LT / RT | ZL / ZR |
| D-pad | D-pad | D-pad |

PC shows the typed code (for example HESOYAM in San Andreas or TURTLE in the GTA 5 console), and Phone shows the number to dial on the in-game phone in GTA 5 and GTA IV. Switch combos only appear for GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas, because those are the games available on Switch through GTA: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition.

### Why do some cheats only show a PC code or phone number?

Not every cheat exists on every input. GTA IV has no button combos at all; every cheat is a phone number such as 482-555-0100 for health, armor and a car repair, and those numbers work identically on every platform. A handful of Vice City cheats, like the character skins (LOOKLIKELANCE, MYSONISALAWYER) and CHICKSWITHGUNS, are PC-only. In GTA 5, the Duke O'Death, Kraken and Dodo are returning-player bonuses that only come as phone numbers or console commands. If a cheat looks blank on your platform, switch to Phone or PC to see how to trigger it.

## Cross-game GTA cheat equivalents

Here is a detail most cheat lists never mention: Rockstar reused a lot of button combos between games. Search a cheat in the finder and compare results across games, and you will spot patterns that make memorising far easier.

### The Rhino tank across every game

The Rhino tank is the classic cross-game search. Type "tank" and you get three results:

| Game | PlayStation | PC |
|---|---|---|
| GTA III | Circle ×6, R1, L2, L1, Triangle, Circle, Triangle | GIVEUSATANK |
| Vice City | Circle, Circle, L1, Circle, Circle, Circle, L1, L2, R1, Triangle, Circle, Triangle | PANZER |
| San Andreas | Circle, Circle, L1, Circle, Circle, Circle, L1, L2, R1, Triangle, Circle, Triangle | AIWPRTON |

Vice City and San Andreas share the exact same tank combo. GTA 5 has no tank cheat at all, but here is the fun part: GTA 5's **Spawn Buzzard** combo (Circle, Circle, L1, Circle, Circle, Circle, L1, L2, R1, Triangle, Circle, Triangle) is identical to the Vice City and San Andreas Rhino. Rockstar swapped the tank for an attack helicopter and kept your muscle memory intact.

### Other combos that carry over between games

- **GTA 5 Sanchez = San Andreas Hunter.** Circle, Cross, L1, Circle, Circle, L1, Circle, R1, R2, L2, L1, L1 spawns a dirt bike in Los Santos and an attack helicopter in San Andreas.
- **GTA 5 Comet = Hotring Racer.** R1, Circle, R2, Right, L1, L2, Cross, Cross, Square, R1 is the GTA 5 Comet and the Hotring Racer A in both Vice City and San Andreas.
- **GTA 5 Slidey Cars = San Andreas Perfect Handling.** Triangle, R1, R1, Left, R1, L1, R2, L1 is the same input with opposite results: ice-rink tyres in GTA 5, glue-like grip in San Andreas.
- **GTA 5 Raise Wanted Level = San Andreas +2 stars.** R1, R1, Circle, R2, Left, Right, Left, Right, Left, Right works in both. GTA 5's Lower Wanted Level is the same opening with the direction pairs reversed (Right, Left, Right, Left, Right, Left).
- **Trashmaster.** Circle, R1, Circle, R1, Left, Left, R1, L1, Circle, Right spawns a garbage truck in GTA 5, Vice City and San Andreas.
- **Vice City and San Andreas share many more**, including Pink Traffic, Black Traffic, Flying Boats, Blow Up All Cars, Pedestrians Riot, Fat and Skinny protagonist, and the Faster Clock. GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas all use the same Faster Clock combo.
- **Flying cars changed in San Andreas.** GTA III (CHITTYCHITTYBB) and Vice City (COMEFLYWITHME) share Right, R2, Circle, R1, L2, Down, L1, R1. San Andreas's RIPAZHA uses a completely different combo.

### The weather cheat pattern

Weather cheats are the clearest example of Rockstar's cheat "grammar". In Vice City and San Andreas, every weather combo starts with **R2, Cross, L1, L1, L2, L2, L2** and only the eighth button changes:

| Final button | San Andreas | Vice City |
|---|---|---|
| Down | Sunny | Very Sunny |
| Triangle | Very Sunny | Sunny |
| Square | Overcast | Overcast |
| Circle | Rain | Rain |
| Cross | Fog | Fog |

Note that Down and Triangle are swapped between the two games. GTA 5's MAKEITRAIN (which cycles through weather types) uses the same seven-button opening and ends on Square, the same input as the Vice City and San Andreas overcast cheat. GTA III uses its own prefix, L1, L2, R1, R2, R2, R1, L2, then Triangle for sunny, Square for cloudy, Circle for rain and Cross for fog. GTA IV handles it with a single phone number, 468-555-0100, that cycles the weather.

## How to read cheat notation

Cheat lists use shorthand, and the finder shows full button icons, but here is the plain-English version so any combo you see online makes sense:

- **L1, R1, L2, R2** are the PlayStation shoulder buttons and triggers. On Xbox they are LB, RB, LT and RT; on Switch they are L, R, ZL and ZR.
- **Up, Down, Left, Right** always mean the d-pad, never the analogue stick.
- **"Circle ×6"** means press Circle six times in a row.
- **PC codes** like HESOYAM are typed during gameplay in GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas with no prompt and no Enter key. In GTA 5 on PC, you open the cheat console with the tilde (~) key, type the word and press Enter.
- **Phone numbers** like 1-999-887-853 are dialled on the in-game phone in GTA 5 and GTA IV. Our [GTA 5 phone cheats guide](/guides/gta-5-phone-cheats/) covers that method in detail.
