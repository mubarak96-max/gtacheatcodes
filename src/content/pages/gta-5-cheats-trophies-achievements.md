## Do GTA 5 cheats disable trophies and achievements?

Yes. The moment you activate any cheat in GTA 5, trophies (PlayStation) and achievements (Xbox and PC) are disabled for the rest of that play session. It doesn't matter how you entered it. A controller combo, a PC console word and a 1-999 phone number all count the same.

The good news is that the block is temporary. It lasts only until you reload a save or restart the game without cheating. Your save file is not permanently tainted, your trophy list isn't touched, and nothing is reported anywhere. Think of it as the game saying "not this time" rather than "never again".

That's the whole answer for most people. The rest of this guide is for players who care about doing it cleanly: platinum and 1000G hunters, completionists, and anyone who wants to mess around with the [GTA 5 cheats](/gta-5-cheats/) without wrecking a 60-hour save.

## How does the GTA 5 cheat trophy lock actually work?

Here's the mechanic in plain terms:

1. **You enter a cheat.** The game confirms it and flags the current session as a cheat session.
2. **Trophies and achievements are blocked.** Anything you'd normally unlock during this session won't pop.
3. **The flag lives in memory, not in the save.** When you load a save or quit and relaunch the game, the flag is cleared.
4. **You're eligible again.** From that point on, as long as you don't cheat, trophies and achievements unlock normally.

A practical consequence: if you cheat, then complete a mission that awards a trophy, you won't get it. Reload, replay without cheats, and it will unlock as normal.

## Does using cheats stop you from saving in GTA 5?

Using cheats does not lock your save file or permanently mark it, and the cheat effects themselves aren't written into the save. Load a save and invincibility, moon gravity and every other active cheat are gone.

What you need to watch is **what** you save. If you save in the middle of a cheat session, you're saving whatever state the world is in: the money you spent, the ammo you burned, the vehicles you parked, the point in the story you reached. That's where people get burned. Details can also differ between versions and platforms, and autosaves can kick in after missions without asking, so don't treat the save system as a safety net.

## Which trophies and achievements are most at risk?

You don't lose anything you've already unlocked. The risk is missing an unlock that would have happened during a cheat session. These categories are worth extra care:

- **Mission completion trophies.** Finishing a story mission while cheating means no trophy for that mission.
- **Gold medal and challenge goals.** Missions are graded on objectives, and replaying them is the usual way to chase gold. Cheat during a replay and any related trophy won't pop.
- **One-time event trophies.** Anything tied to a specific story moment or a first-time action can be awkward to recreate later. Don't cheat when you're near one.
- **Completion percentage.** Cheating doesn't stop collectibles or activities from counting toward completion as far as we know, but the trophy for hitting the target won't unlock in a cheat session.

The simple rule: if you're close to unlocking something, play that stretch clean.

## How to cheat in GTA 5 without risking trophies

This is the routine experienced trophy hunters use. It costs one extra save slot and saves a lot of pain.

1. **Make a clean save.** Before you touch a cheat, save to a slot you'll treat as your "real" progress.
2. **Make a second save in another slot.** This is your sandbox. Name it in your head: the chaos slot.
3. **Load the sandbox and cheat freely.** Moon gravity, explosive punches, a Buzzard at every intersection.
4. **When you're done, reload your clean save.** The session flag clears and trophies are back on.
5. **Never overwrite the clean slot during a cheat session.** If you save, save to the sandbox.

On PlayStation, you can add an extra layer by backing up your save to cloud storage or a USB drive if your setup supports it. On Xbox and PC, saves typically sync to the cloud automatically, which is convenient but means a bad save syncs too. Your backup slot is the real protection.

## Do phone cheats and PC console commands count too?

Yes. This is a common hope, and it's not true. The phone numbers covered in [GTA 5 phone cheats](/guides/gta-5-phone-cheats/) are cheats, and so are PC console commands. GTA 5 doesn't care about the input method. Any cheat activation disables trophies and achievements for that session.

## Do the rules differ between PS3, PS4, PS5, Xbox and PC?

The core rule is the same everywhere: cheats disable trophies or achievements for the session. GTA 5 has shipped on PS3, Xbox 360, PS4, Xbox One, PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the cheat behavior hasn't changed in any way that matters to trophy hunters.

A few practical differences to know:

- **PS4 and PS5 trophy lists can be separate.** If you move from PS4 to PS5, check whether you're earning on a new list. Cheating blocks trophies the same way on both.
- **Xbox 360, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S** all block achievements during a cheat session. How your achievements are listed across generations is down to Xbox, not cheats.
- **PC players** see achievements through their storefront or launcher, depending on where they bought the game. The cheat rule applies regardless.

If you're unsure about a specific edition, the safe approach is the same: keep a clean save and don't cheat right before a trophy.

## Does cheating in Story Mode affect GTA Online?

No. Story Mode cheats don't work in GTA Online, and Story Mode trophies and achievements are separate from Online progress. That separation is deliberate. Cheating in Online with mod menus or third-party tools breaks the rules, risks a ban and ruins the game for other players. Keep the chaos in single-player, where it belongs.
